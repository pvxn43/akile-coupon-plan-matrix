## What the coupon centre refuses, and the trap inside "Apply to server"

The coupon centre is explicitly limited to VPS products. It **excludes Light Cloud, dedicated servers and AI Gateway**, and individual coupons add their own plan, billing-cycle and stacking rules. If the plan you are eyeing sits in a Lite or Light category, check the selector before you pay rather than assuming the code applies.

Coupons come in three shapes (fixed amount, minimum spend and percentage) and two renewal modes, and the renewal mode is where money gets lost. A **One-time renewal** coupon reduces a single invoice. An **Ongoing renewal discount** comes with an "Apply to server" action: you pick an existing server in a dialog, confirm, and the coupon is redeemed immediately while that server's renewal price changes permanently. There is no undo. Read the mode label before you press apply, because a 20% ongoing discount on a machine you plan to keep for years is worth far more than the same 20% taken once, and the reverse is also true if you apply it to a box you are about to cancel.

First-purchase promotions can also exclude existing subscriptions and upgrades. Plan changes happen at your request, may reprice the service, and custom configurations go through a support ticket and quote.

Renewal runs automatically three days before expiry and needs enough balance in the account. If the payment period lapses, the service is deleted automatically and irreversibly. Traffic Auto Renew is a separate switch that deducts a reset fee from your balance when the allowance runs out, listed at ¥7.00. Traffic counts in both directions, upload plus download, which halves the usable figure on a 1TB plan if you are pushing as much as you pull. Entry plans ship with 5GB of disk, tight for anything beyond a proxy, a small site or a log-light service.

## FAQ

**Do I paste the code in a promo box at checkout?**
No. Redeem it first under Finance Center → Coupons → "Redeem a code", then select the resulting coupon in the Select Purchase Time block on the order page while signed in.

**Why is my code not showing as available?**
Four common causes: the coupon has expired or been revoked, the plan or billing term does not match its rules, the product sits in an excluded category such as Light Cloud, dedicated servers or AI Gateway, or an unpaid order already holds it as "Reserved by order".

**Can I stack two codes on one order?**
Each coupon carries its own stacking rules and the order page exposes one selector, so assume one coupon per invoice until the breakdown shows otherwise. A coupon deduction and an annual billing discount are not the same mechanism and can combine on an eligible annual order.

**Will my renewal price go up after the first term?**
It depends on the coupon mode. A One-time renewal coupon cuts one invoice only. An Ongoing renewal discount applied through "Apply to server" permanently rewrites that server's renewal price at the moment you confirm. A coupon used on a new order behaves according to its own rules, which is why the mode label is worth reading before payment.

**Is the 30% code better than the 25% one?**
They cover different products. The 30% applies to LAX Pro on annual billing; the 25% applies to US LAX4837 plans with a 30-minute usage window after redemption. Pick the plan first, then the code that matches it.
